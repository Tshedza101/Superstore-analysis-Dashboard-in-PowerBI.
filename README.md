# Superstore-analysis-Dashboard-in-PowerBI.
End to end Power BI project using sample superstore dataset from Kaggle. This power Bi project aims to unearth the secrets hidden within the superstore dataseet which will in turn help steer the company to profitability.

***Project Objectives***
This power BI dashboard objectives is to address key business questions or objectives through analysis and visualization of the dataset.

##Problem Statement

1. Create Metrics - sales, profit,% of returned orders, show % change py.
2. compare sales perfomance versus previous year time.
3. Determine the most profitable product and the most loss making product.
4. Find the place where most of the profit is happening.
5. sales by segment

**Solution**
1. Used a **Card** Visual to create metrics of Sales, profit and % returned orders and compare it with previous year metrics respectively with percentage change.
2. Used a **line chart** on the dashboard to compare this year sales vs previous sales, and in the dashboard its clealy visible that the present year sales are better than previous year sales which shows growth in the sales department.
3. From the clustered column chart in the dashboard, **Technology copiers** are the most profitable products while **Furniture tables** are the most loss making products.
4. California is where most of the profit is happening as shown from the **Shape map** in the dashboard.
5. From the **Donut chart** in the dashboard, *Consumer segment* has the highest sales with 50.32%, followed by *Corporate* with 30.77%, and finally by *Home office* with 18.92%.
